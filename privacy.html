Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

MaxWanted Poster ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own social media accounts.

What data the app uses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth and Meta login), the app uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts and reads basic channel information and video statistics (views, retention, likes).
  • The app does not collect, sell or share data from any other person.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, with restricted file permissions. No data is sent to third parties other than the platforms themselves (Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram) through their official APIs.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions or in Meta's Business Integrations settings.
